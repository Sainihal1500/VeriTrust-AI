VERICOMMERCE - TRANSACTION SECURITY POLICY

Document ID: PAYMENT-SECURITY-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. AUTHORIZED PAYMENT EVIDENCE

Payment, refund, and charge status must come from the authorized transaction system. A customer statement or bank notification may prompt an investigation but does not by itself establish VeriCommerce transaction status.

2. SENSITIVE PAYMENT DATA

Never request or record a full card number, CVV, PIN, online banking password, UPI PIN, or OTP. Use transaction references and masked account details where available.

3. FRAUD CONCERNS

Suspected unauthorized charges or account misuse should be routed through the designated fraud-support process. Do not state that fraud is confirmed before specialist review.

4. REFUND INTEGRITY

Only an authorized refund record confirms the refund amount, status, destination method, or estimated completion. Do not fabricate or guarantee transaction activity.
