VERICOMMERCE - UPI PAYMENT POLICY

Document ID: PAYMENT-UPI-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. UPI AVAILABILITY

UPI is available when shown as an option at checkout and supported by the customer's payment application and bank.

2. PENDING UPI REQUESTS

Customers should approve payment only inside their trusted payment application. A pending UPI request is not proof of a successful VeriCommerce payment. The authorized transaction record is the source of truth for order payment status.

3. FAILED UPI PAYMENTS

If a UPI payment fails or remains pending after a debit, customers may contact support with the order ID and the transaction reference shown in the authorized payment record. Reversal timing depends on the payment provider and bank; no fixed timeline should be guaranteed.

4. SAFE UPI USE

Customers must not share a UPI PIN, password, OTP, or approval code with a support agent. VeriCommerce support will not ask the customer to approve an unexpected collect request or install remote access software.
