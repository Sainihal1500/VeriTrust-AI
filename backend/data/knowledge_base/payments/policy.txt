VERICOMMERCE - PAYMENT POLICY

Document ID: PAYMENT-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. PAYMENT METHODS

VeriCommerce may support multiple authorized payment methods including:
- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- Authorized digital payment methods
- Bank-based payment methods
- Other payment methods displayed during checkout

The payment methods available to a customer depend on the checkout system and applicable eligibility requirements.

2. PAYMENT AUTHORIZATION

An order may require successful payment authorization before it can proceed to fulfillment.

A payment authorization does not by itself guarantee that an order has been shipped.

Customer-service agents must verify the order and payment records before providing payment-related information.

3. FAILED PAYMENTS

A payment may fail because of:
- Bank or payment-provider rejection
- Incorrect payment information
- Insufficient available funds
- Temporary payment-provider problems
- Security verification requirements
- Technical processing problems

Agents must not assume the exact reason for a failed payment unless the authorized payment system provides that information.

4. PAYMENT PENDING

A payment may remain in a pending state while the payment provider completes processing.

Customers should not be told that a payment has permanently failed when the authorized payment system reports a pending status.

The agent must use the current payment status provided by the authorized system.

5. DUPLICATE PAYMENT

If a customer reports that they were charged more than once, the agent must verify the payment records.

Two payment entries do not automatically mean that the customer was permanently charged twice.

One transaction may be an authorization or temporary hold.

Agents must not promise a refund for a duplicate payment until the payment records have been verified.

6. PAYMENT REVERSAL

A payment authorization may be reversed or released by the payment provider.

The exact time required for a reversal or release depends on the payment provider and transaction status.

Customer-service agents must not promise an exact reversal time unless the authorized payment system provides one.

7. REFUNDS TO PAYMENT METHODS

When a refund is approved, the refund is normally processed through the applicable original payment method or another authorized method supported by the payment system.

The refund processing period must be determined using the applicable refund policy and payment information.

Agents must not claim that a refund has been completed unless the authorized system confirms the refund status.

8. PAYMENT INFORMATION SECURITY

Customer-service agents must never request or disclose:

- Full payment card numbers
- Card security codes
- PINs
- Passwords
- One-time authentication codes
- Account recovery codes
- Internal payment credentials

Only the minimum information necessary for authorized verification should be requested.

9. PAYMENT STATUS VERIFICATION

For questions about a specific payment, the system must use the authorized payment record.

The knowledge base provides general payment procedures but does not contain the live status of individual transactions.

The system must not invent:
- Payment status
- Transaction IDs
- Payment amounts
- Refund status
- Authorization status
- Bank responses

10. CURRENCY AND PAYMENT AMOUNTS

The final amount charged to a customer must be obtained from the authorized order or payment system.

Agents must not calculate or guess a customer's final payment amount when an authoritative transaction record is available.

11. PAYMENT DISPUTES

If a customer disputes a payment, the agent must verify the transaction and follow the applicable dispute and escalation procedure.

If the system cannot verify the transaction, the case should be escalated rather than answered using assumptions.

12. CUSTOMER PRIVACY

Payment information is confidential customer information.

Payment details may only be disclosed after appropriate customer verification.

Information belonging to another customer must never be disclosed.

13. VERITRUST AI REQUIREMENT

VeriTrust AI must distinguish between general payment policy and live payment information.

General payment questions should use trusted payment policies.

Questions about a specific transaction must use an authorized payment data source.

If the required payment information cannot be verified, VeriTrust AI must clearly state that the information cannot currently be verified.

The system must never fabricate payment information.
