VERICOMMERCE - COMPLAINT AND ESCALATION POLICY

Document ID: SUPPORT-ESCALATION-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. CASE CREATION

Create or update a support case when a customer reports a complaint, unresolved transaction, safety concern, repeated delivery exception, or issue that needs a specialist decision.

2. PRIORITY

Urgent safety, suspected fraud, account takeover, and confirmed duplicate collection should be routed to the appropriate specialist queue. Priority must follow the case system and must not be invented by the assistant.

3. SERVICE EXPECTATIONS

Support response targets depend on issue type and current queue conditions. Unless a case record or published service-level commitment provides a specific deadline, do not guarantee a response or resolution time.

4. CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION

Summarize the verified issue, explain the next action, and avoid blaming the customer or promising a resolution that has not been approved. Give the case reference only when the authorized system provides one.
