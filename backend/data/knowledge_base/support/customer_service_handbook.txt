VERICOMMERCE CUSTOMER SERVICE HANDBOOK

Document ID: CS-HANDBOOK-001
Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Source Type: Official Internal Policy

==================================================
1. GENERAL CUSTOMER SERVICE
==================================================

VeriCommerce customer service agents must provide information
based only on approved company policies, customer records,
product information, and authorized operational systems.

Agents must not invent:
- Prices
- Discounts
- Delivery dates
- Refund eligibility
- Warranty coverage
- Product specifications
- Account information
- Order status
- Payment status

When information cannot be verified, the agent must clearly
state that the information cannot currently be verified.

==================================================
2. CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION
==================================================

Customer-specific information may only be disclosed after
appropriate account verification.

Agents must never disclose:
- Full payment card numbers
- Passwords
- Authentication codes
- Internal security credentials
- Another customer's information

==================================================
3. ORDER INFORMATION
==================================================

Customers may request information about their own orders.

Available order information includes:
- Order number
- Order date
- Product name
- Product quantity
- Order status
- Shipment status
- Estimated delivery information
- Payment status

Agents must use the authorized order database when answering
questions about a customer's specific order.

==================================================
4. ORDER STATUS
==================================================

Supported order statuses include:

PLACED
PAYMENT_PENDING
PAYMENT_CONFIRMED
PROCESSING
PACKED
SHIPPED
OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
DELIVERED
CANCELLED
RETURN_REQUESTED
RETURNED
REFUND_PENDING
REFUNDED

Agents must not claim that an order has shipped unless the
authorized order system confirms SHIPPED or a later status.

==================================================
5. ORDER MODIFICATION
==================================================

Customers may request changes to an order before the order
enters the PACKED status.

After an order enters PACKED status, modification may no longer
be possible.

After an order has entered SHIPPED status, the delivery address
cannot normally be changed through customer service.

Customers should contact support for eligible exceptions.

==================================================
6. ORDER CANCELLATION
==================================================

Orders may normally be cancelled before they enter SHIPPED status.

Cancellation requests after shipment require evaluation by
customer service.

A shipped order cannot be represented as cancelled unless the
authorized order system confirms cancellation.

==================================================
7. DELIVERY
==================================================

Standard domestic delivery generally takes 3-7 business days
after shipment.

Express delivery generally takes 1-3 business days after shipment.

Delivery estimates are estimates and are not guaranteed unless
a specific service agreement states otherwise.

Agents must use the shipment system for customer-specific
delivery information.

==================================================
8. DELAYED SHIPMENTS
==================================================

If an order has exceeded its expected delivery window, the agent
should check the shipment tracking record.

Agents must not invent a new delivery date.

If tracking information is unavailable, the customer should be
informed that the shipment status cannot currently be verified.

==================================================
9. LOST SHIPMENTS
==================================================

A shipment may be classified as potentially lost when tracking
has not updated for the period defined by the logistics team.

Customer service must not declare a shipment lost without
confirmation from the authorized logistics process.

Once a shipment is officially classified as lost, eligible
replacement or refund procedures may be initiated.

==================================================
10. DAMAGED PRODUCTS
==================================================

Customers who receive a visibly damaged product should contact
customer service and provide the requested evidence.

Evidence may include photographs of:
- Product damage
- Packaging damage
- Shipping label
- Product identification information

Customer service determines the next step according to the
damage and returns policies.

==================================================
11. REFUNDS
==================================================

Standard refunds are governed by the Refund Policy.

Customers may request a standard refund within 30 calendar days
of the original purchase date.

Requests submitted more than 30 calendar days after purchase
are not eligible for a standard refund.

Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after
approval.

Exceptions require approval from a customer service supervisor.

Agents must not promise an exception before approval.

==================================================
12. RETURNS
==================================================

Return eligibility depends on the product, purchase date,
condition of the product, and applicable return policy.

A return request does not automatically guarantee a refund.

Customers should receive return instructions only after the
applicable eligibility requirements have been verified.

==================================================
13. WARRANTY
==================================================

Warranty coverage depends on the product and applicable
warranty terms.

Agents must not promise warranty coverage without checking the
applicable product warranty information.

Damage caused by misuse may be excluded from standard warranty
coverage.

==================================================
14. PAYMENTS
==================================================

Supported payment methods may include:
- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- Authorized digital payment methods
- Other payment methods explicitly listed during checkout

The currently supported methods must be verified against the
active payment configuration.

==================================================
15. PAYMENT FAILURES
==================================================

If a payment fails, the order may remain in PAYMENT_PENDING
status.

Customers should not be told that an order is confirmed unless
the payment system confirms successful payment.

Customers should never be asked to provide their full card
number, PIN, password, or authentication code to customer
service.

==================================================
16. PROMOTIONS
==================================================

Promotional discounts are subject to their individual terms.

A promotional code may have:
- An expiration date
- Product restrictions
- Minimum purchase requirements
- Customer eligibility requirements
- Usage limits

Agents must verify the promotion before confirming eligibility.

==================================================
17. ACCOUNT SECURITY
==================================================

Customers are responsible for protecting their passwords and
authentication credentials.

Customer service agents will never request a customer's password.

Authentication codes must never be requested through ordinary
customer-service chat.

==================================================
18. PRIVACY
==================================================

Customer information must only be used for authorized business
purposes.

Agents must not disclose personal information belonging to
another customer.

Sensitive information must be minimized in customer-service
responses.

==================================================
19. ESCALATION
==================================================

Issues should be escalated when:
- Policy information conflicts
- Required evidence is unavailable
- A supervisor exception is required
- A security concern is detected
- A customer disputes an important account or transaction fact
- The requested action requires authorization beyond the agent

==================================================
20. VERITRUST RESPONSE RULE
==================================================

Every factual customer-service response should be grounded in
trusted company evidence.

When trusted evidence contradicts the generated response,
the generated response must not be shown to the customer.

The response must be corrected, re-verified, or escalated.

When no reliable evidence exists, the system must not guess.

END OF DOCUMENT
