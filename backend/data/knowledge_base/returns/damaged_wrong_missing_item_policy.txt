VERICOMMERCE - DAMAGED, WRONG, OR MISSING ITEM POLICY

Document ID: RETURN-ISSUES-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. REPORTING AN ISSUE

Customers should report a damaged item, incorrect product, or missing item through VeriCommerce support as soon as reasonably possible. Support may request the order ID, a description, and relevant photographs when permitted by the case procedure.

2. VERIFICATION

The authorized order, product, and shipment records must be checked before confirming that an item was damaged, incorrect, or missing. A report does not by itself establish eligibility for a replacement, refund, or reimbursement.

3. RESOLUTION

Available resolutions depend on the verified issue, product, shipment, and applicable policy. Agents must not promise a replacement, refund, free return shipping, or reimbursement before approval is recorded.

4. CUSTOMER SAFETY

Customers should stop using a product if continued use may create a safety concern and contact support for instructions. Do not ask a customer to perform unsafe repairs or discard the item before the case instructions are confirmed.
