VERICOMMERCE - CUSTOMER PRIVACY POLICY

Document ID: PRIVACY-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. PRIVACY PRINCIPLES

VeriCommerce protects customer information and limits access to information required for legitimate customer-service, operational, security, and legal purposes.

Customer information must only be accessed and disclosed through authorized systems and procedures.

2. CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Customer information may include:
- Name
- Contact information
- Shipping address
- Billing information
- Order history
- Payment status
- Support interactions
- Return and refund information
- Account information

Access to customer information must be limited to authorized purposes.

3. CUSTOMER VERIFICATION

Before providing customer-specific account, order, payment, return, refund, or warranty information, the customer must be appropriately verified.

Verification requirements depend on the type and sensitivity of the information being requested.

Agents must not bypass required verification procedures.

4. ANOTHER CUSTOMER'S INFORMATION

Customer-service agents must never disclose information belonging to another customer.

This includes:
- Names
- Addresses
- Phone numbers
- Email addresses
- Order information
- Payment information
- Account information
- Support records

If the requested information cannot be safely verified, the agent must not disclose it.

5. PAYMENT INFORMATION

Sensitive payment information must be protected.

Agents must never request or disclose:
- Full payment card numbers
- Card security codes
- PINs
- Passwords
- One-time authentication codes
- Account recovery codes

Only information necessary for authorized verification should be requested.

6. PASSWORDS AND CREDENTIALS

Customers must never be asked to provide their password to customer-service personnel.

Agents must not request internal credentials, authentication tokens, security codes, or other confidential credentials.

If an account-access issue occurs, the customer should be directed through the approved account recovery process.

7. SHIPPING INFORMATION

A customer's shipping address is confidential customer information.

Customer-specific shipping information must only be disclosed after appropriate verification.

An address belonging to another customer must never be disclosed.

8. ORDER HISTORY

Order history is customer-specific information.

Agents may access or disclose order information only when authorized and after the required customer verification.

The system must not reveal another customer's order history.

9. DATA MINIMIZATION

The customer-service system should request and process only the information necessary to complete the requested support task.

Unnecessary sensitive information must not be requested.

10. CUSTOMER SUPPORT RECORDS

Support interactions may contain personal or account-related information.

Support records must be accessed only for authorized customer-service or operational purposes.

Agents must not expose internal notes or confidential operational information to customers unless specifically authorized.

11. DATA ACCURACY

Customer information should be obtained from authorized systems.

Agents must not invent or modify customer information.

If information appears incorrect, the issue should be handled through the applicable account or support procedure.

12. DATA DISCLOSURE

Customer information may only be disclosed through authorized procedures.

Requests for sensitive customer information that cannot be verified must be refused or escalated according to the applicable procedure.

13. DATA SECURITY

Customer information must be protected against unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or misuse.

Security incidents involving customer information must follow the applicable security escalation process.

14. ACCOUNT ACCESS

If a customer cannot access their account, customer-service agents must follow the approved account recovery procedure.

Agents must not bypass account-security controls.

15. PRIVACY REQUESTS

Customers may submit privacy-related requests through the authorized VeriCommerce privacy process.

The customer-service system must not make legal or regulatory determinations unless an authorized policy or process explicitly permits it.

Complex or sensitive privacy requests should be escalated to the appropriate privacy or compliance team.

16. DATA RETENTION

Customer information may be retained according to applicable company retention requirements.

Customer-service agents must not promise immediate deletion or a specific retention period unless the applicable policy explicitly provides it.

17. INTERNAL INFORMATION

Internal systems, credentials, security procedures, confidential business information, and internal operational records must not be disclosed to customers unless specifically authorized.

18. AI SYSTEM PRIVACY

VeriTrust AI must not expose private customer information that is not necessary to answer the customer's request.

The AI system must use authorized data sources and respect access controls.

Customer-specific information must not be inferred or fabricated.

19. PRIVACY INCIDENTS

If the system or an agent detects a possible unauthorized disclosure, privacy incident, or security incident, the case must be escalated according to the applicable security and privacy procedure.

Agents must not conceal or invent information about an incident.

20. VERITRUST AI REQUIREMENT

VeriTrust AI must treat customer-specific information as protected information.

The system must:

- Verify customers before providing sensitive information.
- Use authorized data sources.
- Never reveal another customer's information.
- Never request passwords or authentication codes.
- Never fabricate personal information.
- Never expose internal confidential information.
- Escalate sensitive privacy requests when required.

If the system cannot safely verify whether information may be disclosed, it must not disclose the information and should provide the appropriate escalation or verification path.
