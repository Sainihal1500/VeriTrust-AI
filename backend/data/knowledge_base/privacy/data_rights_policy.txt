VERICOMMERCE - DATA ACCESS AND DELETION POLICY

Document ID: PRIVACY-RIGHTS-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. CUSTOMER REQUESTS

Customers may contact support to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, subject to identity verification, legal obligations, and information required to complete transactions or resolve disputes.

2. VERIFICATION

Sensitive data requests must be submitted through the authenticated account or another approved identity-verification process. Do not disclose or delete data based only on a name, email address, order ID, or natural-language claim.

3. DELETION REVIEW

Deletion requests may require review because some records must be retained for accounting, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or active support cases. A request is not complete until an authorized privacy workflow confirms completion.

4. DATA MINIMIZATION

Use and disclose only the personal information needed for the approved customer-service purpose. Never include passwords, OTPs, payment security codes, or full payment credentials in support notes.
