VERICOMMERCE - PASSWORD AND OTP SAFETY POLICY

Document ID: SECURITY-CREDENTIALS-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. SECRET CREDENTIALS

Customers must never share passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), UPI PINs, card PINs, CVVs, recovery codes, or authentication approval codes with VeriCommerce staff or an AI assistant.

2. SUPPORT VERIFICATION

Support must not ask a customer to read, send, paste, or approve an OTP to verify identity. Use the approved authenticated session and verification workflow.

3. PASSWORD RESET

Customers should use the official account recovery flow. Support may direct a customer to that flow but must not request the current password or set a password on the customer's behalf.

4. SUSPICIOUS CONTACT

Do not follow unexpected links, approve an unsolicited payment request, install remote-access tools, or share device access. Report suspected account compromise through the official support channel.
