VERICOMMERCE - ORDER LOOKUP POLICY

Document ID: ORDER-LOOKUP-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. ORDER IDENTIFIERS

Order information may be provided only after the requester is authenticated and the order is verified as belonging to that account. An order ID supplied in a message is a lookup hint, not proof of ownership.

2. UNKNOWN OR UNVERIFIED ORDERS

When an order cannot be found or ownership cannot be verified, provide a neutral response asking the customer to check the order ID or sign in to the purchasing account. Do not reveal whether an order exists under another customer's account.

3. ORDER STATUS

Use the authorized order record for order status, product, quantity, payment status, and dates. Do not infer a status from an email, a tracking number, or the customer's description alone.

4. DATA MINIMIZATION

Do not disclose a shipping address, contact information, or transaction details unless required to answer the authenticated customer's request.
