VERICOMMERCE - REPLACEMENT POLICY

Document ID: WARRANTY-REPLACE-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. ELIGIBILITY

Replacement eligibility depends on the product, issue, purchase record, stock, return inspection, and applicable policy. A reported defect or damaged delivery does not automatically approve a replacement.

2. APPROVAL AND MODEL

Only an authorized case or order record can confirm replacement approval and the replacement item. Do not promise a particular model, color, or equivalent item unless the record confirms it.

3. TIMING

Replacement processing and shipping times depend on availability and fulfillment. Agents must not promise a delivery date that is not confirmed by an authorized shipment record.

4. REFUND ALTERNATIVE

A replacement request and a refund request are distinct resolutions. The customer must be told which option is recorded as approved; do not imply that both will be provided.
