VERICOMMERCE - WARRANTY POLICY

Document ID: WARRANTY-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. WARRANTY COVERAGE

Warranty coverage depends on the product, applicable warranty terms, purchase date, and the condition of the product.

The warranty period for a specific product must be verified using the applicable product information or warranty record.

Agents must not assume that every product has the same warranty period.

2. WARRANTY START DATE

Unless a product-specific policy states otherwise, the applicable warranty period is generally calculated from the original purchase date.

The authorized order or purchase record should be used when the purchase date must be verified.

3. COVERED DEFECTS

Warranty service may apply to defects covered by the applicable product warranty.

Examples of potentially covered issues may include manufacturing defects or failures occurring under normal intended use, subject to the specific warranty terms.

A customer-service agent must not confirm that a particular failure is covered until the applicable warranty conditions have been verified.

4. EXCLUDED DAMAGE

Warranty coverage may not apply to damage caused by circumstances excluded by the applicable warranty terms.

Possible exclusions may include:
- Accidental damage
- Misuse
- Improper installation
- Unauthorized modification
- Unauthorized repair
- Physical damage
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage caused by incompatible accessories
- Damage caused by use outside specified operating conditions

The exact exclusions depend on the applicable product warranty.

5. NORMAL WEAR AND TEAR

Normal wear and tear is not automatically considered a manufacturing defect.

Warranty eligibility must be determined according to the applicable product warranty.

Agents must not guarantee warranty coverage based only on a customer's description.

6. PROOF OF PURCHASE

A valid purchase record may be required to verify warranty eligibility.

The authorized order or customer account system should be used when available.

Agents must not invent purchase dates or warranty status when the information cannot be verified.

7. WARRANTY CLAIM

A customer requesting warranty service may need to provide information about:
- Product
- Order or purchase information
- Problem description
- Relevant photographs or evidence
- Product serial number where applicable

Only information necessary for the warranty process should be requested.

8. WARRANTY INSPECTION

Some warranty claims may require inspection or technical evaluation.

Submitting a warranty claim does not automatically guarantee approval.

The final warranty determination must follow the applicable product warranty terms and authorized evaluation process.

9. REPAIR

Where warranty service includes repair, the available repair process depends on the product and applicable warranty.

Agents must not promise a specific repair completion date unless an authorized system or service agreement provides one.

10. REPLACEMENT

Where the applicable warranty permits replacement, the replacement process must follow the authorized warranty procedure.

Agents must not promise a specific replacement product, model, color, or delivery date unless confirmed by an authorized system.

11. REFUND AND WARRANTY

A warranty claim and a standard refund request are separate processes.

A warranty issue does not automatically mean that a refund will be issued.

Refund eligibility must be determined according to the applicable refund policy and authorized approval process.

12. WARRANTY EXPIRATION

A warranty request submitted after the applicable warranty period is not automatically eligible for warranty service.

Any exception must be evaluated according to the applicable policy and authorization process.

Agents must not promise an expired warranty exception without authorization.

13. PRODUCT-SPECIFIC WARRANTY

Different products may have different warranty periods and conditions.

When a customer asks about a particular product, the system must retrieve the applicable product warranty information whenever available.

General warranty information must not override product-specific warranty terms.

14. SECOND-HAND OR TRANSFERRED PRODUCTS

Warranty eligibility for products purchased or transferred outside the original authorized purchase process must be determined according to the applicable warranty terms.

Agents must not assume that warranty coverage transfers automatically.

15. CUSTOMER-CAUSED DAMAGE

If evidence indicates that damage may have resulted from misuse, accidental damage, unauthorized modification, or another excluded condition, the claim must be evaluated under the applicable warranty terms.

Agents must not independently declare a claim fraudulent or invalid without the appropriate evaluation process.

16. WARRANTY STATUS

For a specific customer warranty request, the system may need to verify:
- Product
- Purchase date
- Warranty period
- Product condition
- Claim status
- Previous service history
- Applicable warranty terms

Customer-specific warranty information must come from authorized records.

17. CUSTOMER PRIVACY

Warranty information associated with a customer's account or order is confidential.

The system must verify the customer before disclosing customer-specific warranty information.

Information belonging to another customer must never be disclosed.

18. VERITRUST AI REQUIREMENT

VeriTrust AI must distinguish between general warranty policy and customer-specific warranty eligibility.

General questions should be answered using trusted warranty and product documents.

Customer-specific questions should use authorized order, product, and warranty records.

If the required information cannot be verified, the system must clearly state that warranty eligibility or status cannot currently be confirmed.

The system must never fabricate:
- Warranty periods
- Warranty status
- Claim approval
- Repair status
- Replacement approval
- Replacement delivery dates
- Inspection results
