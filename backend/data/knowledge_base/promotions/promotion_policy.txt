VERICOMMERCE - PROMOTION AND DISCOUNT POLICY

Document ID: PROMO-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. PROMOTION ELIGIBILITY

Promotional offers are available only when the customer and order satisfy the conditions specified for the promotion.

Eligibility may depend on:
- Promotion dates
- Eligible products
- Customer eligibility
- Minimum purchase requirements
- Payment method
- Geographic restrictions
- Quantity limits
- Other stated promotion conditions

2. PROMOTION PERIOD

Every promotion has a defined validity period.

A promotion is only valid during the period specified by the applicable promotion terms.

Agents must not extend a promotion beyond its stated validity period unless an authorized exception exists.

3. PROMOTION CODES

Some promotions require a valid promotion or coupon code.

A promotion code must be applied according to the applicable promotion terms.

Agents must not invent promotion codes or claim that an invalid code is valid.

4. ONE-TIME PROMOTIONS

Some promotions may be limited to one use per customer or account.

The customer's eligibility must be verified using the authorized promotion system when required.

Agents must not assume that a customer is eligible for a one-time offer without verification.

5. MINIMUM PURCHASE REQUIREMENTS

Some promotions require the customer to reach a minimum eligible purchase amount.

Only eligible products and charges specified by the promotion terms may count toward the minimum.

The applicable promotion system should be used when eligibility depends on the customer's actual order.

6. PRODUCT EXCLUSIONS

Certain products or categories may be excluded from promotions.

The applicable promotion terms must be checked before confirming that a product qualifies.

Agents must not assume that a discount applies to every product.

7. DISCOUNT CALCULATION

The final discount must be calculated using the authorized checkout or promotion system.

Agents must not manually invent or guarantee a discount amount when the authorized system can determine the actual value.

8. STACKING PROMOTIONS

Promotions may or may not be combined with other discounts.

Whether multiple offers can be used together depends on the applicable promotion terms.

Agents must not promise that two promotions can be combined unless the terms explicitly allow it.

9. FREE SHIPPING PROMOTIONS

Free shipping promotions may have specific:
- Minimum purchase requirements
- Eligible destinations
- Eligible products
- Shipping methods
- Validity periods

The applicable promotion terms must be checked before confirming eligibility.

10. PROMOTIONAL PRODUCTS

Products included in promotional offers may have additional conditions related to returns, refunds, or cancellations.

The applicable promotion and product policies must be reviewed when necessary.

11. PROMOTION AND RETURNS

Returning a product purchased using a promotion may affect the promotional discount or eligibility.

The applicable return and promotion terms must be followed.

Agents must not promise that the original promotional discount will remain unchanged after a partial or complete return.

12. PROMOTION AND CANCELLATION

Cancelling an order may affect promotional eligibility.

If a promotion required a minimum purchase or multiple qualifying products, cancellation of part of the order may change the customer's eligibility.

The authorized order and promotion systems should be checked when necessary.

13. PROMOTION AND REFUNDS

A refund associated with a promotional purchase must follow the applicable refund and promotion policies.

The refund amount must be based on the authorized transaction record.

Agents must not promise a specific refund amount without verifying the transaction.

14. EXPIRED PROMOTIONS

An expired promotion is not automatically available.

Agents must not reactivate an expired promotion unless an authorized system or policy explicitly permits it.

15. PROMOTION ERRORS

If a customer believes a promotion was not applied correctly, the order and promotion records should be checked.

The agent must not immediately promise an adjustment without verifying the promotion conditions.

16. PROMOTIONAL PRICE DISPLAY

The final price shown by the authorized checkout system should be treated as the authoritative transaction amount.

Marketing or promotional material must not override the actual authorized order record.

17. CUSTOMER-SPECIFIC PROMOTION ELIGIBILITY

Questions such as:

"Am I eligible for this discount?"

may require customer-specific information.

The system should use the authorized promotion system where applicable.

General promotion documents must not be used to invent individual customer eligibility.

18. PROMOTION FRAUD AND ABUSE

Suspected misuse or abuse of promotional offers must be handled according to the applicable security and escalation procedure.

Customer-service agents must not accuse a customer of fraud without an authorized investigation or determination.

19. PROMOTION DATA

The system must not fabricate:
- Promotion codes
- Discount percentages
- Discount amounts
- Eligibility
- Promotion expiry dates
- Minimum purchase requirements
- Product eligibility

20. VERITRUST AI REQUIREMENT

VeriTrust AI must distinguish between general promotion rules and live promotion eligibility.

General promotion questions should use trusted promotion documents.

Customer-specific promotion questions should use authorized promotion or order data when available.

If the system cannot verify current promotion information, it must clearly state that the information cannot currently be verified.

The AI must never invent a promotion or promise a discount that is not supported by trusted evidence.
