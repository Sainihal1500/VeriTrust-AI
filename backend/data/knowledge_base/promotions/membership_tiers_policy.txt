VERICOMMERCE - MEMBERSHIP TIER POLICY

Document ID: PROMO-MEMBER-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. TIERS

VeriCommerce demo accounts may have Standard, Silver, Gold, or Platinum membership tiers. The authenticated customer record is the source of truth for an individual tier.

2. BENEFITS

Membership benefits may include eligible promotions or service options, but benefits vary by offer and are not guaranteed for every product or order. Check the active promotion terms before describing a discount or delivery benefit.

3. TIER CHANGES

Tier status may change when account eligibility criteria are met. Agents must not promise an upgrade date or a specific benefit unless the customer record or an authorized offer confirms it.

4. PRIVACY

Customer-specific membership information may be disclosed only to the authenticated customer associated with the account.
