VERICOMMERCE - COUPON AND PROMOTION CODE POLICY

Document ID: PROMO-COUPON-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. COUPON TERMS

Each coupon has its own validity dates, eligible products, minimum order value, customer eligibility, payment conditions, and redemption limits. The promotion record and checkout are authoritative.

2. APPLYING A CODE

Customers should enter a coupon at checkout before placing the order. A displayed discount is confirmed only when it appears in the final order total.

3. STACKING AND TRANSFER

Coupons may not be combined unless the applicable promotion terms explicitly allow it. A coupon assigned to one account must not be represented as transferable without confirmation.

4. FAILED OR EXPIRED CODES

If a code is rejected, support may check its status and eligibility. Agents must not invent replacement codes, extend expiry dates, or promise a discount after purchase without authorized approval.
