VERICOMMERCE - PRODUCT INFORMATION POLICY

Document ID: PRODUCT-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. PRODUCT INFORMATION PRINCIPLE

Product information provided to customers must come from an authorized VeriCommerce product catalog or trusted product documentation.

Agents and AI systems must not invent product specifications.

2. PRODUCT CATEGORIES

VeriCommerce products may be organized into categories including:

- Smartphones
- Laptops
- Tablets
- Audio devices
- Wearable devices
- Computer accessories
- Mobile accessories
- Home electronics

The actual products and specifications must be obtained from the authorized product catalog.

3. PRODUCT IDENTIFICATION

A product should be identified using reliable information such as:
- Product ID
- SKU
- Model number
- Product name
- Authorized catalog information

If a customer provides an incomplete or ambiguous product name, the system should request clarification rather than guessing.

4. PRODUCT NAME

The official product name must be obtained from the authorized catalog.

Agents must not create unofficial product names.

5. PRODUCT PRICE

The current price of a specific product must be obtained from the authorized product or pricing system.

Prices may change over time.

Agents must not claim that a price is current unless the authorized system provides the current price.

6. PRODUCT AVAILABILITY

Product availability must be obtained from the authorized inventory or product system.

Possible inventory states may include:
- IN_STOCK
- LOW_STOCK
- OUT_OF_STOCK
- PRE_ORDER
- DISCONTINUED

Agents must not claim that a product is available without verifying current inventory information.

7. PRODUCT COLORS

Available colors must be obtained from the authorized product catalog.

Agents must not assume that every model is available in every color.

8. PRODUCT STORAGE OPTIONS

Products may have multiple storage or configuration options.

The exact configurations must be obtained from the authorized product catalog.

Agents must not invent storage capacities or configurations.

9. PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS

Product specifications may include:
- Dimensions
- Weight
- Display characteristics
- Processor
- Memory
- Storage
- Battery capacity
- Camera specifications
- Connectivity
- Ports
- Operating system
- Included accessories

All specifications must come from trusted product information.

10. BATTERY INFORMATION

Battery capacity and charging capabilities must be obtained from the authorized product documentation.

Agents must not estimate battery capacity.

Claims about charging speed must only be made when supported by trusted product information.

11. DISPLAY INFORMATION

Display-related information must come from the authorized product documentation.

Examples include:
- Screen size
- Resolution
- Refresh rate
- Display technology
- Brightness

Agents must not invent specifications.

12. PROCESSOR AND PERFORMANCE

Processor, chipset, memory, storage, and performance-related specifications must come from the authorized product catalog.

Agents must not claim benchmark results unless those results are explicitly available in an authorized source.

13. COMPATIBILITY

Compatibility information must be based on trusted product documentation.

Examples include:
- Operating system compatibility
- Accessory compatibility
- Charging compatibility
- Network compatibility
- Software compatibility

Agents must not guarantee compatibility based solely on assumptions.

14. ACCESSORIES

Included accessories must be determined using the specific product configuration.

Optional accessories must not be described as included unless the product documentation confirms this.

15. PRODUCT WARRANTY

Warranty information must be obtained from the applicable warranty policy and product-specific warranty documentation.

The general warranty policy does not automatically determine the warranty period for every product.

16. PRODUCT CONDITION

Products may have different conditions such as:
- New
- Refurbished
- Open-box
- Clearance

The product condition must be obtained from the authorized catalog.

Agents must not describe a product as new, refurbished, or open-box without verification.

17. PRODUCT AVAILABILITY AND ORDERING

A product being listed in the catalog does not necessarily mean it is currently available for purchase.

Current availability must be checked using the authorized inventory or ordering system.

18. PRODUCT REVIEWS

Customer reviews and ratings must not be represented as official product specifications.

If review information is presented, it must be clearly distinguished from official product information.

19. PRODUCT COMPARISONS

When comparing products, the system should use verified specifications from authorized product information.

The system must not invent differences between products.

If a specification is unavailable for one of the products, the system should clearly state that the comparison cannot be verified for that specification.

20. DISCONTINUED PRODUCTS

If a product is discontinued, the system should rely on the authorized catalog status.

Agents must not claim that a discontinued product is available unless the inventory system confirms availability.

21. PRODUCT QUESTIONS WITH AMBIGUOUS NAMES

If multiple products have similar names, the system should ask the customer for additional identifying information such as:
- Model
- SKU
- Product ID
- Configuration

The system must not guess which product the customer means.

22. CURRENT PRODUCT INFORMATION

Product specifications are relatively stable, but prices, availability, configurations, and promotions may change.

For current information, the authorized live product or inventory system should be preferred when available.

23. VERITRUST AI REQUIREMENT

VeriTrust AI must distinguish between:

GENERAL PRODUCT INFORMATION:
Use trusted product documentation.

CURRENT PRODUCT DATA:
Use an authorized product or inventory system when available.

CUSTOMER-SPECIFIC PRODUCT ORDERS:
Use the authorized order system.

The system must never fabricate:
- Product specifications
- Product prices
- Product availability
- Product colors
- Storage configurations
- Warranty periods
- Compatibility
- Included accessories

If the required product information cannot be verified, the system must clearly state that the information is unavailable or requires verification.
