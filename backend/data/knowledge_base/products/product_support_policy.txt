VERICOMMERCE - PRODUCT SUPPORT POLICY

Document ID: PRODUCT-SUPPORT-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. TRUSTED PRODUCT DETAILS

Product names, specifications, compatibility, stock, and prices must come from the current VeriCommerce catalog or approved product documentation. Do not infer compatibility from a product category alone.

2. COMPATIBILITY QUESTIONS

Check the specific product's listed ports, protocols, operating-system support, and accessory requirements. If a device or configuration is not listed, say that compatibility cannot be confirmed and offer support review.

3. TROUBLESHOOTING

Offer only safe, documented setup guidance. Do not instruct customers to open power supplies, bypass safety controls, or perform repairs that could cause injury or void warranty terms.

4. PRODUCT AVAILABILITY

Catalog stock is a current demo-data value and may change. A stock count does not reserve an item or guarantee availability at checkout.
