VERICOMMERCE - CANCELLATION PROCESSING POLICY

Document ID: CANCEL-PROCESS-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. REQUEST AND CONFIRMATION

A cancellation request is not complete until the authorized order system records the order as cancelled. Customers should check the order status after submitting a request.

2. FULFILLMENT STATUS

Orders may be cancelled when fulfillment has not progressed beyond the stage permitted by the order system. Once a package has shipped, cancellation is normally unavailable; the customer may need to follow the applicable return process after delivery.

3. PAYMENT AND REFUND

If payment was collected before cancellation, the payment and refund records determine whether funds were captured and whether a refund was initiated. Cancellation alone does not establish refund completion.

4. CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION

Agents must not claim that an order has been cancelled or a refund issued unless the authorized record confirms that status.
