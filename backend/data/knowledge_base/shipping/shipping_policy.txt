VERICOMMERCE - SHIPPING POLICY

Document ID: SHIPPING-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. STANDARD DELIVERY

Standard domestic delivery generally takes 3-7 business days after the order has been shipped.

The delivery estimate is an estimate and may vary because of carrier operations, weather, holidays, remote locations, operational disruptions, or other circumstances.

Agents must not guarantee a delivery date unless an authorized system or customer agreement explicitly provides a guaranteed date.

2. EXPRESS DELIVERY

Eligible orders may have an express delivery option.

Express delivery generally takes 1-3 business days after shipment.

Availability and delivery estimates depend on the destination, product eligibility, carrier service, and checkout options.

3. ORDER PROCESSING TIME

Shipping time begins after the order has been processed and handed to the carrier.

Order processing before shipment is separate from carrier transit time.

Agents must not tell a customer that an order is in transit when the authorized order system shows that it has not yet been shipped.

4. SHIPPING STATUS

The following statuses may be used:

LABEL_CREATED:
A shipping label has been generated but the package may not yet have been handed to the carrier.

IN_TRANSIT:
The carrier has received the package and it is moving through the carrier network.

OUT_FOR_DELIVERY:
The carrier indicates that delivery is expected during the current delivery cycle.

DELIVERED:
The carrier reports that the package has been delivered.

DELAYED:
The shipment has exceeded its expected delivery window or the carrier has reported a delay.

5. TRACKING INFORMATION

When available, tracking information must be obtained from the authorized order or shipping system.

Agents must not invent tracking numbers or carrier information.

If a tracking number has not yet been generated, the agent must clearly state that tracking information is not currently available.

6. DELIVERY ESTIMATES

Delivery estimates depend on:
- Destination
- Shipping method
- Order processing
- Carrier operations
- Product availability
- Holidays
- Weather
- Operational disruptions

An estimated delivery date must not be presented as a guaranteed delivery date unless the authorized system explicitly identifies it as guaranteed.

7. DELAYED SHIPMENTS

If a shipment is delayed, the agent must check the latest authorized tracking information.

The agent may explain the reported status and applicable company procedure.

The agent must not invent a reason for a delay.

If the carrier has not provided a reason, the response must not claim a specific cause.

8. LOST SHIPMENTS

A shipment must not be declared lost solely because it is late.

The applicable lost-shipment procedure should be followed using the carrier information and company policy.

If investigation is required, the case may need to be escalated.

Agents must not promise a replacement or refund unless the applicable policy and authorized system permit it.

9. DAMAGED SHIPMENTS

If a package arrives damaged, the customer should report the issue through the applicable support process.

The agent may request information or evidence that is permitted by the support procedure.

The agent must not promise reimbursement or replacement before eligibility has been verified.

10. DELIVERY ADDRESS CHANGES

Address changes should be requested before shipment whenever possible.

Once an order has been shipped, an address change normally cannot be guaranteed.

The agent must check the current order and shipping status before providing an answer.

11. DELIVERY ATTEMPTS

A carrier may make one or more delivery attempts depending on the carrier's procedures.

If delivery cannot be completed, the package may be held, returned, or scheduled for another attempt according to the carrier's procedures.

Agents must use the available tracking information rather than guessing what the carrier will do.

12. PACKAGE MARKED DELIVERED BUT NOT RECEIVED

If tracking reports a package as delivered but the customer cannot locate it, the customer-service agent must follow the applicable investigation procedure.

The agent must not immediately state that the package was stolen or lost without verified evidence.

13. INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING

International shipping may have different delivery times and requirements.

Customers may be responsible for applicable customs duties, taxes, or other charges where required by the destination and applicable terms.

International delivery estimates must not be represented as guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed by an authorized system or agreement.

14. SHIPPING COSTS

Shipping charges must be obtained from the authorized checkout or order system.

Agents must not invent shipping charges.

If a promotional shipping offer applies, the applicable promotion terms must also be verified.

15. CARRIER INFORMATION

The carrier associated with a specific shipment must be obtained from the authorized shipping or order system.

General knowledge of common carriers must not be used to guess the carrier for a customer's order.

16. VERICOMMERCE AI REQUIREMENT

VeriTrust AI must distinguish between general shipping policy and live shipment information.

General shipping questions should use the trusted shipping policy.

Questions about a specific shipment must use an authorized order or shipping data source.

The system must never fabricate:
- Tracking numbers
- Carrier names
- Shipment status
- Delivery dates
- Delivery attempts
- Shipment locations
- Reasons for delays

If current shipment information cannot be verified, the system must clearly state that it cannot verify the current status.
