VERICOMMERCE - DELIVERY DELAY POLICY

Document ID: SHIPPING-DELAY-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. CHECK THE LATEST STATUS

For a delayed delivery, use the latest authorized carrier or shipment update. An estimated delivery date may change and is not a delivery guarantee.

2. DELAY REASONS

Do not guess why a package is delayed. State a reason only when the carrier or authorized operations record provides it.

3. PACKAGE MARKED DELIVERED

If tracking shows delivered but the customer cannot locate the parcel, follow the delivery investigation procedure. Do not declare theft or loss without verification.

4. ESCALATION

If tracking is stale, contradictory, or indicates a delivery exception, create or update a support case for review. Do not promise replacement or refund before eligibility is approved.
