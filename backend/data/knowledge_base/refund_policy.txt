VERICOMMERCE - CUSTOMER REFUND POLICY

Document ID: REFUND-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. Refund Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of the original purchase date.

2. Refund Requests After 30 Days

Refund requests submitted more than 30 calendar days after purchase are not eligible for a standard refund.

3. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval.

4. Exceptions

Exceptions to the standard refund policy require approval from a customer service supervisor.

5. Important Rule

Customer-service agents must not promise a refund unless the request satisfies the eligibility requirements or an authorized supervisor has approved an exception.
